title: "Multi-instance launches get CEF profile slots"
timestamp: 2026-11-11T16:34:28.694198449+00:00
---
Anarc allows up to 9 concurrent windows. The installed launcher wrapper (/usr/local.. no: ~/.local/bin/anarc generated by install.sh) tries flock slots 1..9 in $XDG_RUNTIME_DIR and exports ANARC_INSTANCE_SLOT for slots 2+. paths::cef_root_dir() maps slot>=2 to <data>/cef/instance-<slot>; slot 1 keeps <data>/cef. Reason: Chromium places its process singleton + cookie/cache databases inside --user-data-dir (= CEF root_cache_path), so two processes sharing it corrupt the profile. Settings, spaces.json, history.sqlite (WAL) and favicons stay shared, matching a second browser window. Request-context profiles remain direct children of the root cache path.
